About me
Hola! I am Marc, your host in Barcelona. I am happy to welcome you to my apartment and make your stay a great experience.
I have extensive expertise in tourism, with a Sustainable Tourism Management degree from Holland. I have worked in several countries across the world, such as England, Costa Rica, Thailand and China, and spent a number of years living and working in Japan. I have enjoyed and learned from all my experiences outside my home town, Barcelona, and I have always felt welcome wherever I went, meeting different cultures and people.
Now I have returned to Barcelona and wish to give this warmth and expertise back to my guests. I hope you enjoy the best of this fascinating and beautiful Mediterranean city, and our wonderful and welcoming apartment!

Owners
Merca Stay for owners
Merca Stay is an initiative to manage and develop tourist apartments within the principles of sustainability. There is a lot to improve in the lodging industry, towards a greener and more respectful present and future. We use the latest know-how and technologies to adapt to the demands of the market, pioneer the industry and attract quality tourism. If you are an owner looking to optimise your revenue while meeting sustainable development goals with a positive impact on the city of Barcelona, Merca Stay is your perfect choice.
