House rules

A few rules for a great stay

Welcome to Merca Stay! We are very happy to share this experience with you and we hope you have an unforgettable stay with us.

Please take a few minutes to read the rules we kindly ask you to respect while you are in the apartment.

  1. Cancellation policy

    Full refund for cancellations made within 48 hours of booking, if the check-in date is at least 30 days away. 50% refund for cancellations made at least 14 days before check-in. No refunds for cancellations made within 14 days of check-in.

  2. Bond

    At check-in a bond of €500 is required. It is returned to you at check-out, after we check that the apartment is in good condition.*

  3. Smoking

    Smoking is only allowed on the balconies.

  4. Keys and intercom

    Always remember to take the keys with you. If you need to ring the intercom, please make sure it is the right one so as not to disturb the neighbours. Do not confuse our building’s entrance with the identical building next door: our entrance is just behind the bus stop.

  5. No parties

    Parties are strictly forbidden. The city has an incredible nightlife, so please don’t throw parties at home! If a neighbour calls us to report noise, we will be forced to ask you to leave, without any refund. No noise from 22:00 onwards, including music, television and the washing machine.

  6. Neighbours

    Please respect the other residents of the building, and don’t make noise in the common areas, especially when going out and coming back at night.

  7. Rubbish

    Rubbish must go in the bins outside the building. It should never be left inside the building or outside the apartment door.

  8. Toilet

    Please don’t throw anything but toilet paper into the WC.

  9. Energy

    Turn off the lights and all electrical devices when you leave the apartment.

  10. Towels

    Please do not take the towels to the beach.

  11. Guests

    No additional guests are allowed under any circumstances without our authorisation.

  12. Belongings

    We are not responsible for any lost or stolen objects left in the apartment.

*Before check-out, please make sure the apartment is in good condition: wash your dishes, throw away your rubbish and leave the towels in the bathroom. To avoid losing your deposit or being charged on your credit card, please behave well. Damage we check after check-out:

  • All appliances: TV, fridge, washing machine, dryer, dishwasher, toaster, coffee machine, air conditioning, electric shutters (up to the entire damage deposit).
  • Loss of keys: €150 (losing the keys means changing the lock).
  • Parties and noise reported by neighbours: loss of the entire amount of the stay.
  • Towels: do not use them as a bath mat, and be careful with make-up (€5 for each small towel damaged and €10 for each large one).
