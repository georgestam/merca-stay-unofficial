Privacy on this site

This is an unofficial redesign concept of merca-stay-apartments.com. It collects nothing.

  • The forms do not send anything. They prepare a message on your device that you can open in your own email app or WhatsApp, or copy. Nothing leaves your browser until you send it yourself.
  • There are no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from this site.
  • Links to Marc’s official booking calendars, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

For bookings made with Merca Stay, read the privacy policy on the official website.
