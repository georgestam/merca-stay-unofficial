Sustainability

Greener stays, by design

Sustainable development is the only way to assure a better future. Our generation has the responsibility to awaken consciousness towards ecology and to respect our natural resources. Making this apartment as eco-friendly as possible is really important to us, so you will find:

  • Electricity

    The apartment is powered by 100% renewable energy (Holaluz). All lights have been replaced by LED bulbs, and household appliances are rated A++ or A+++. The air conditioning shuts down if a window is opened.

  • Gas

    Gas is still a non-renewable energy, so it remains one of our goals for the future. The boiler has been replaced by a much more efficient one. We hope to install solar panels on the rooftop of the building to replace the gas installation for hot water, and are waiting for the community’s approval.

  • Noise

    It is crucial to respect the neighbours. Staying in an apartment building lets you live like a local in Barcelona, but please remember that your neighbours are not on holiday. We have installed a decibel sensor that warns us when too much noise is made inside the apartment. Parties are strictly forbidden.

  • Recycling

    Waste is separated into the 5 types ordered by Barcelona City Council. The containers are just outside the apartment, on the street. When we refurbish, old furniture in good condition is given to an NGO (Cáritas).

  • Mobility

    We recommend walking the city, with everything easily reachable on foot. You can also rent a bike (we can recommend a neighbourhood bike rental) or take public transport: 2 metro stations are within 5 minutes’ walk.

  • Signs

    There is information inside the apartment explaining how best to use energy.

  • Amenities

    We provide complimentary wine, fruit, shampoo and soap, all of them eco-friendly.

  • Paper

    Kitchen paper and toilet paper are eco-friendly and biodegradable.

  • Laundry

    We use Fundació Roure, an NGO that employs Barcelona citizens at risk of socio-economic exclusion.

  • Decoration

    We promote local artists by using the apartments’ walls to exhibit and sell their art. Contact us if you are interested in any of the artwork.

  • Certification

    We are implementing the good practices recommended by Biosphere Responsible Tourism, and our goal is to obtain their certificate of recognition.

Merca Stay follows the good practices of Biosphere Responsible Tourism.
