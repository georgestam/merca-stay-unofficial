# Merca Stay: original adapted showreel prompt

Make a dynamic 60-second motion-graphics video for Jordi's affectionate, unofficial tribute to Marc Mercadé and Merca Stay Apartments. Make it feel like a motion designer's best showreel: minimalist and tasteful, with bold, memorable transformations and a genuine wow factor.

The idea is “The art of doing absolutely nothing. Professionally hosted. Ridiculously overthought.” Invent a fictional Holiday Department where a suitcase runs logistics, the sun handles lighting and a cushion chairs the meeting. Make the bureaucracy of relaxing the joke. Marc is the person being warmly celebrated, not impersonated.

Use original abstract illustrations, oversized typography, architectural arches, a travel tag, a geometric sun, waves, an expressive suitcase and a very self-important cushion. Use a warm Mediterranean palette: cream, ink, sun yellow and terracotta. Match cuts should turn an inbox into a suitcase, calendar blocks into buildings, an arch into a sun and a spreadsheet into sea waves. Make the movement crisp and satisfying, with confident pauses that let every punchline land.

Follow the eight-scene storyboard below, exactly 7.5 seconds per scene. Use only the specified short on-screen phrases. Always leave comfortable margins and allow at least two seconds to read a punchline. Compose in 16:9 at 1920 × 1080, with the essential action kept within the centre for mobile viewing. No flashing cuts or strobing.

Use an original instrumental sound bed and gently comic, synthesised clicks, stamps and soft whooshes. No voice imitation or unlicensed music. This version needs no narration. Text and visual timing must tell the complete story with sound off.

Do not use a real person's face, cloned voice, invented quotation, customer review, star rating, price, promise about an apartment or claim of approval. Barcelona and Sitges are the only location claims. Original illustrations evoke travel, not the appearance of an actual property. Keep “Unofficial affectionate parody” legible in a small, persistent corner label. Finish on “Made by Jordi. With affection.”

## Sixty-second storyboard

| Time | On-screen copy | Graphic action and comic beat |
|---|---|---|
| 00.0–07.5 | YOU HAVE / 1 NEW HOLIDAY. | A severe little inbox appears. Its envelope folds into a cream suitcase. The notification dot becomes a sunny yellow travel tag. Hold on “HOLIDAY”. |
| 07.5–15.0 | MARC, / WE MADE YOU / A DEPARTMENT. | A terracotta stamp lands. A clean organisation chart grows from it, then rearranges into three absurdly grand office doors. No portrait. |
| 15.0–22.5 | THE SUN / HANDLES LIGHTING. | One door opens into an arch. The arch turns into a rising geometric sun. Its rays click into place like a studio lighting rig. A tiny switch receives a very important click. |
| 22.5–30.0 | BARCELONA. / OUT OF OFFICE. | Calendar blocks unfold into an abstract Mediterranean city. A tiny “meeting” rectangle slides away. A coffee cup arrives with the visual gravitas of a major product launch. |
| 30.0–37.5 | SITGES. / INTO THE BLUE. | The coffee cup's rim expands into a circular horizon. City lines stretch into sunlit waves. A small suitcase reclines beneath a perfectly oversized parasol. |
| 37.5–45.0 | THE CUSHION / WILL CHAIR / THIS MEETING. | A cushion bounces onto a dignified chair. An agenda unfolds: just one large zero. The cushion settles. Give the stillness a full beat. |
| 45.0–52.5 | YOUR FIVE-YEAR PLAN? / FIVE MORE MINUTES. | A grand timeline scrolls across the screen, then curls into a hammock. Five chunky dots bob slowly. One tries to rush ahead, then returns to its place. |
| 52.5–60.0 | MERCA STAY / THE ART OF DOING / ABSOLUTELY NOTHING. / Made by Jordi. With affection. | Sun, arch, waves and suitcase assemble into a polished final composition. The travel tag swings once and settles. The final three seconds are a readable end card with the parody label. |
