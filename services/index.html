Services

Everything arranged before you land

From the airport to a flamenco show to a quiet evening out while a babysitter reads bedtime stories. Ask Marc and he will confirm availability and price.

Request a service

  • Additional cleaning

    All of our apartments are cleaned by a professional cleaning company before check-in. If you are staying longer, or simply love the feeling of being in a hotel, we offer additional cleaning during your stay: daily, or once a week. It’s your choice!

    Request this: Additional cleaning

  • Transfers

    Luggage, long taxi queues in summer and the language barrier: the beginning of your holiday does not sound that thrilling. This is why we offer transfers from Barcelona airport and the city’s main train stations to your accommodation. It is the best way to start your trip relaxed and well looked after.

    Request this: Transfers

  • Tablao Flamenco Cordobés

    The only tablao on the Rambla, open since 1970. An icon of flamenco in Spain, founded and run by a family of artists, and recently recognised as the best tablao flamenco in Spain.

    Request this: Tablao Flamenco Cordobés

  • Luggage storage

    Checking out in the morning with a flight late at night? Our luggage service picks up your suitcases from the apartment and gives them back to you at the airport. It is the simplest and most secure way to explore freely and enjoy your last moments in the city.

    Request this: Luggage storage

  • Bicycle rental

    Cycling is a great way to discover Barcelona. Our supplier is a 1 minute walk from the apartment and has a wide range of bicycles. Show a Merca Stay business card for a discount.

    Request this: Bicycle rental

  • Tours

    Tours and activities from Montserrat to the Dalí Museum, Barcelona city tours, PortAventura theme park and more.

    Request this: Tours

  • Music school

    While you are staying in Barcelona we offer intensive and extensive guitar courses in English and Spanish. At Estudio Shine, the Shine School of Music offers guitar classes with qualified, experienced teachers, for students of all ages and levels.

    Request this: Music school

  • Babysitter

    Take some time away from the children and see how a real holiday feels. Our babysitters are trained professionals who speak Spanish and English. They make sure your children have the time of their lives, in complete safety.

    Request this: Babysitter
