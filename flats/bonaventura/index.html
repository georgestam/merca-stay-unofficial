About the apartment

  • 73 m²

A first-floor apartment facing the street, with 3 bedrooms, 1 full bathroom, a living and dining room, a fully equipped kitchen and 2 balconies.

Spacious at 73 square metres, very sunny and very well located in Sitges, on a quiet street near the bus and train stations. It has 3 bedrooms: the main bedroom with a double bed, another with two single beds, and a third with two 105 cm beds. Fully equipped kitchen (washing machine, dishwasher, oven, microwave, ceramic hob, coffee maker, toaster, juicer), a full bathroom with shower, two balconies and air conditioning. The apartment has been renovated and is in a historic building with few neighbours.
