We offer a spacious (200 m²) and comfortable home away from home. Located on an upper floor (with lift), the apartment is bright and airy, and you can enjoy typical Barcelona views from the balconies. The apartment offers a new concept of art and sustainability. The building's neighbours are quiet and there is a concierge.

Situated in the Eixample, it is a 4 minute walk to Passeig de Gràcia, the main street, and a 12 minute walk to the Sagrada Família. Two metro stops are close by: Diagonal and Verdaguer.

Good to know

The street
Carrer del Bruc is named after the Battles of El Bruc in June 1808, when Catalan militia beat Napoleon's army twice near Montserrat.
The legend
A drummer boy's echoing drum is said to have fooled the French into thinking a whole army lay in wait. A Catalan history journal thinks the tale was borrowed from a battle in 1794.
Quiet by design
Besides two bathrooms there is a separate toilet, and a decibel sensor alerts Marc when it gets too loud.
