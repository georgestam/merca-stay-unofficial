This apartment has 3 double bedrooms, a spacious living and dining room, a fully equipped kitchen and a full bathroom with a bathtub. It is a comfortable apartment just a few metres from the beach, right in the heart of Sitges on its most popular street.

Thanks to the central location, you and your loved ones will have everything within easy reach. The bedrooms have aluminium windows that block out street noise. 75 m², up to 6 people.
