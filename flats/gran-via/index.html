About the apartment

  • Sleeps 8
  • 3 double bedrooms, one en suite
  • Sofa bed for 2
  • Open kitchen with island
  • Lift
  • 2 metro stops to Passeig de Gràcia

A new and elegant flat on Gran Via, at the corner with Padilla. It is next to the Monumental, the Teatre Nacional and the new Plaça de les Glòries.

The flat has a double bedroom en suite and two more double bedrooms. In the living room two more people can stay on a sofa bed, so in total it sleeps 8. The dining room is very bright, with an open kitchen with an island, a dining table and the living room. The location is great: you are 2 metro stops from Passeig de Gràcia, the main shopping street, and Plaça Catalunya (L1 or L2).

Good to know

The longest street
Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes runs for about 13.1 km, the longest street in Catalonia.
The Monumental
Next door, the Monumental bullring held its last bullfight in 2011. There has not been one since.
Els Encants
The Encants flea market at Glòries, a short walk away, trades under a huge mirrored roof.
